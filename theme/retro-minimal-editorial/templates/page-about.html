I am a software engineer and designer crafting deliberate digital tools, editorial publishing systems, and tactile web interfaces.

I believe digital spaces should feel tactile, fast, and respectful of human attention. In an era dominated by bloated JavaScript bundles and noisy feeds, I lean into neo-brutalism and classic editorial typography: crisp 1px borders, honest ink-on-paper contrast, generous reading rhythm, and instantaneous load times.

My work combines classical software craftsmanship with frontier AI velocity. Rather than using AI as an indiscriminate code generator, I treat frontier models as tireless apprentices—enabling rapid vibe-coding, high-fidelity prototyping, and uncompromising typographic systems.
